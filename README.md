# Nutrition Fitness Tracker

[www.nutrition-fitness-tracker.ca/](https://www.nutrition-fitness-tracker.ca)

Advanced monitoring utility for fitness, bodyweight, body composition, and caloric predictions and analysis.

---

## Overview

Nutrition Fitness Tracker is a comprehensive web application designed to help users monitor and optimize their fitness and health through data-driven insights. By analyzing weight changes, nutrition history, and body composition measurements, the application provides precise caloric recommendations and tracks key health metrics over time.

---

## Features

### Caloric Prediction & Analysis
- Dynamic calorie recommendations tailored for weight loss, weight gain, and maintenance goals
- Algorithmic predictions based on historical weight fluctuations and nutrition data
- Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) calculations
- Activity level predictions and activity multiplier determination based on maintenance calories and BMR

### Body Composition Tracking
- US Navy method body fat percentage calculations
- Fat-free (lean) mass and fat mass tracking over time
- Body composition visualizations and trend analysis
- Bodyweight predictions at various body fat percentage intervals

### Health Indicators
- Body Mass Index (BMI) calculator
- Fat Free Mass Index (FFMI) calculator
- Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) calculator
- Comprehensive health metric monitoring

### Nutrition Analysis
- Macronutrient tracking and analysis
- Caloric representation of consumed macronutrients
- Nutrition history logging

### Data Visualization
- Interactive plots and graphs for all tracked metrics
- Historical trend analysis
- Progress tracking across multiple dimensions

---

## How It Works

The application combines multiple data points to provide accurate, personalized recommendations:

1. **Weight Tracking**: Records bodyweight changes over time to establish trends
2. **Nutrition Logging**: Captures daily caloric and macronutrient intake
3. **Composition Analysis**: Uses US Navy calculations to determine body fat percentage and lean mass
4. **Algorithmic Processing**: Analyzes the relationship between caloric intake and weight changes to predict individual metabolic response
5. **Activity Assessment**: Determines physical activity levels based on the difference between maintenance calories and BMR
6. **Personalized Recommendations**: Generates precise caloric targets for specific weight management goals
